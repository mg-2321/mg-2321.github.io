Projects Blogs

I’m a second-year M.S. student in the Computer Science department at the University of Washington. Currently, my research supervised by Dr. Ruta Desai focuses on Assistive Agents: planning and teaching language models when to ask vs. when to act, with lightweight grounding via a knowledge-augmented graph (KAG) and prefrence optimization algorithms(SimPO, KTO, ORPO) to choose the best model based on preference-satisfaction rate.

Additionally, I'm exploring how to overcome memory context-window limitations using short- and long-term retrieval caches, summarization, and Reflexion-style episodic notes that are indexed and reused across tasks.

I’m open to research collaborations. Please email me (gayat23 [at] uw.edu) if you are interested to explore assistive agents for day-to-day task automation, tool use, XR copilots, and self-evolving language models for curriculum generation, reflective fine-tuning, and preference optimization.

Preference Optimization Algorithms: SimPO vs SDPO

We compare two preference-optimization methods for aligning LLMs. SimPO directly maximizes a margin between preferred and dispreferred responses with a stabilizing reference model. SDPO attributes credit step-wise along trajectories to reduce reward hacking and improve reasoning. We outline practical tips, when to prefer each, and open questions.
