Projects Quick look

I’m a second-year M.S. student in the Computer Science department at the University of Washington. Currently, my research supervised by Dr. Ruta Desai focuses on agentic AI: planning and teaching language models when to ask vs. when to act, with lightweight grounding via a knowledge-augmented graph (KAG) and preference-based training using SimPO and ORPO.

Additionally, I’m exploring how to overcome short-term memory limits for long-horizon tasks using retrieval caches, summarization/compression, and Reflexion-style episodic notes that are indexed and reused across tasks with a Neo4j-backed KAG.

I’m open to research collaborations. Please email me on gayat23[at]uw.edu if you’re interested in assistive agents for day-to-day automation and self-evolving language models for curriculum generation, reflective fine-tuning, and preference optimization.
