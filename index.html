I am,
Gayatri Malladi

I’m a second-year M.S. student in the Computer Science department at the University of Washington. My research with Dr. Ruta Desai explores agentic AI: planning and teaching language models when to ask vs. when to act, with a knowledge-augmented graph (KAG) and preference-based training (SimPO, ORPO).

I’m working on overcoming short-term memory limits for long-horizon tasks via retrieval caches, summarization/compression, and Reflexion-style episodic notes indexed in a Neo4j-backed KAG.

If you’d like to collaborate, email: gayat23[at]uw.edu.
